Payment Institution Actor

PSD2 Annex I · maturity unknown

Open Business Blueprint for a Payment Institution (PSD2 Annex I): money remittance, payment initiation services (PIS), account information services (AIS), and execution of payment transactions on a payment account. This repository publishes a governed payment-institution actor as an OSS business that any qualified, licensed operator can fork, deploy, run, improve and sell.

Built on this workspace's langgraph-clj StateGraph runtime (portable .cljc, supervised superstep loop, interrupts, in-mem checkpoints) -- the same actor pattern as cloud-itonami-isic-6419 (Banking Advisor ⊣ Monetary Intermediation Governor) and cloud-itonami-isic-6910 (Registrar-LLM ⊣ RegistrarGovernor). Here it is PaymentOps-LLM ⊣ PIGovernor.
