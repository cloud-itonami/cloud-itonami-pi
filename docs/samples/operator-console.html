Action gate (PIGovernor)

HARD holds cannot be overridden by a human approver. Spec-basis, evidence completeness, IBAN checksum, sanctions status and PIS-consent presence are independently recomputed, never trusted from the advisor's proposal; a real payment execution or remittance payout is always a human operator's call, at every rollout phase.

OpGate
:account/intakephase-3 auto-commit when clean, no actuation yet -- the ONLY auto-eligible op in this domain, at any phase
:compliance/verifyALWAYS human approval · spec-basis independently checked against pi.facts, never fabricated
:sanctions/screenALWAYS human approval when clean · an unresolved sanctions flag is a HARD, un-overridable hold instead
:consent/register-pis / :consent/register-aisALWAYS human approval · requires a real spec-basis citation, never fabricated
:actuation/execute-paymentALWAYS human approval · real payment execution (actuation) · evidence-completeness + IBAN checksum + sanctions + (PIS channel) consent independently verified, never auto at any phase
:actuation/remit-payoutALWAYS human approval · real remittance payout (actuation) · double-payout guard independently enforced, never auto at any phase
